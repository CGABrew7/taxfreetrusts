Estate and gift tax · not income-tax magic

Most trusts are not tax-free. The useful ones are specific.

In 2026 the federal estate and gift exemption is $15 million per person, $30 million for a married couple. Above that, the rate is 40 percent. Below that, an irrevocable tax trust is usually the wrong purchase.

Education only. Not legal, tax, or investment advice. A trust used to hide income or lie to the IRS is a crime. We will not help with that. Confirm figures on IRS.gov.

Insurance

ILIT

Trust owns the policy so the death benefit is generally outside the taxable estate if lookback rules are respected.

Appreciation

GRAT

You keep an annuity for a term. Growth above the 7520 hurdle can pass with little gift tax if you survive the term.

Spouse access

SLAT

Uses exemption now. The other spouse may receive distributions. Reciprocal trusts and divorce are failure modes.

Sale

IDGT

You sell an asset to the trust on a note. You pay the income tax, which is not an extra gift under current law.

Charity

CRT

Income stream, remainder to charity. Used for a concentrated position with real charitable intent.

House

QPRT

You keep the right to live in the house for a term. Discounted remainder if you survive the term.
