What a living trust actually does

A revocable living trust is a bucket with your name on it while you are alive. At death the successor trustee hands it to the people you listed without a probate file for those assets.

It does not cut paycheck tax. It does not replace a will for guardians. The failure mode is an unfunded house still in your individual name.

This article is education. It is not legal advice.
