When a trust saves tax. When it does not.

A revocable living trust does not reduce federal income tax. Do not retitle an IRA into the trust.

Federal estate tax in 2026 is widely reported at $15 million per person. Most families never touch it. State estate tax in Illinois, New York, Massachusetts, and Washington is the sleeper.

A large life policy in your name can land in the taxable estate. An ILIT is counsel work, not the $349 packet.
