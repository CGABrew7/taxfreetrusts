A draft desk for estate plans.

TaxFreeTrusts drafts wills and living trusts from your answers, at prices posted on the page.

What the desk does

The desk turns five questions into a packet you can print, sign and fund. It covers the common case well: a home, some accounts, children and maybe a shop or a farm. When a matter is larger than the packet, the desk says so and introduces you to an attorney.

The method

  1. You answer the planner: who the plan is for, what moves and who runs it.
  2. The planner recommends one packet by a fixed rule. A large life policy or a business that may grow, with a net worth band of $5M or more, points to counsel. Every other answer points to the living trust. The will is always offered.
  3. You pay the posted price on a Stripe Payment Link.
  4. You print the packet, sign it using your state's formalities and fund the trust from the funding list.

What you will not find here

No ads. No affiliate links. No phone line. No subscriptions. Three prices, each paid once.

Where your answers go

Nowhere. The planner runs in your browser tab and keeps your answers there. Close the tab and they are gone.
