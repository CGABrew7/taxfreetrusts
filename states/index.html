Questions

What is on each state page?

Each page covers the probate path, fees, the small-estate procedure, estate or inheritance tax, and how spouses hold property, but only where a statute or a court site states the point. A missing point is left off the page.

Does the calculator invent a fee?

No. It multiplies a statutory schedule when the page has one. If the statute says reasonable compensation and gives no percentage, the calculator does not invent a dollar fee.
