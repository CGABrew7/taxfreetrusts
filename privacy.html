Privacy policy

Hanok Ventures, operating TaxFreeTrusts. Updated 23 September 2026.

This is the working privacy policy for taxfreetrusts.com. It describes a small static site that sells document drafts. It is not a substitute for a lawyer's review if the product later stores accounts, drafts, or more customer data.

Who we are

Hanok Ventures operates this site as TaxFreeTrusts. Questions about privacy go to plan@taxfreetrusts.com.

What we collect

  • Email you send us. If you write plan@taxfreetrusts.com, we receive your address, your message, and any files you attach.
  • The planner on the start page. Names, state, and notes you type stay in that browser tab so a draft can print. This site does not send that form to our server. Closing the tab drops it, unless you email it to us or include it in a note on Stripe.
  • Payments. Will ($79), living trust ($349), and counsel desk ($790) are charged on Stripe Payment Links. You enter the card on Stripe. We do not store full card numbers on this site. Stripe may give us the name, email, and amount needed to deliver the order.
  • Server logs. Cloudflare hosts the site and keeps ordinary request logs, such as IP address, browser, page, and time.

How we use it

We use this information to deliver a draft, reply to support, and keep the site running. We do not use it to build an advertising profile.

Who we share it with

Stripe processes payments. Cloudflare hosts the site. We do not sell personal information. We do not share it for cross-context advertising. We share it if the law requires, for example a valid legal demand.

Cookies and analytics

These pages do not set their own cookies and do not load an analytics or advertising tag. There is no Google Analytics, Meta pixel, or similar tool in the site.

The pages do request typefaces from Google Fonts, so your browser contacts Google for those files. Stripe's checkout page is a separate site and follows Stripe's own policy. Cloudflare may log requests as part of hosting.

California requests

If you are a California resident, you can email plan@taxfreetrusts.com to ask what personal information we have about you, to ask us to delete or correct it, and to opt out of any sale or sharing. We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. We will confirm the request is yours and respond as the law requires. We will not discriminate against you for asking.

Children

The site is for adults making an estate plan. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. A parent may type a child's name into the planner. That text stays in the browser unless the parent sends it to us.

Security

The site is served over HTTPS. Card numbers stay with Stripe, not on these pages. Email and hosting logs are not a vault. No method of sending data is perfect.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will post the new version on this page and update the date above.
