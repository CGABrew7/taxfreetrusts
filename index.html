A trust only controls what you move into it.

A signed trust with nothing in it keeps nothing out of probate. The living trust packet comes with a funding list, so the deed and the accounts actually move.

How funding works

Funding listSCHEDULE A
  1. Deed to the homeRecorded into the trust
  2. Checking and savingsRetitled, or payable on death
  3. Brokerage accountRetitled, or transfer on death
  4. Retirement accountsBeneficiaries reviewed, never retitled
  5. Life policyBeneficiary confirmed
