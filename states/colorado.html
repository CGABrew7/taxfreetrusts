Living trust or probate

A revocable living trust holds assets you title in the trustee's name. You can serve as trustee while you are able.

At death, the successor trustee handles those assets. Property you leave in your own name can still go through the probate court. A pour-over will sends leftover assets into the trust, and those leftover assets may pass through probate first.

The probate path

In Colorado, the estate is opened in the District Court, or Denver Probate Court in Denver.

Colorado Judicial Branch probate forms are captioned for District Court or Denver Probate Court. Informal probate instructions say the probate registrar appoints the personal representative instead of the judge, and that the Information of Appointment must be given within 30 days of appointment. A proposed probate-rule draft on the judicial branch site says the probate rules govern procedure in the probate court for the city and county of Denver and in district courts when sitting in probate. The opened materials do not set out creditor-claim or final-distribution deadlines.

Source: Colorado Judicial Branch, Information of Appointment, informal probate instructions

Fees

In Colorado, probate compensation is reasonable compensation under the statute linked below.

Colorado Revised Statutes section 15-12-719, Compensation of personal representative, is repealed. Section 15-10-602(1) says a fiduciary and his or her lawyer are entitled to reasonable compensation for services rendered on behalf of an estate. The court determines reasonableness, and a person who receives excessive compensation must make an appropriate refund.

Source: Colorado Revised Statutes section 15-10-602, Recovery of reasonable compensation and costs

Small estate

Colorado's small-estate figure on this page is $88,000 when the statute's conditions are met.

Section 15-12-1201 allows collection of personal property by affidavit at any time ten or more days after death. The affidavit must state that the fair market value of property owned by the decedent and subject to disposition by will or intestate succession at the time of death, wherever located, less liens and encumbrances, does not exceed twice the amount set forth in section 15-11-403, as adjusted by section 15-10-112. The Colorado Department of Revenue form DR 2712, revised 04/30/26 and published 05/01/26, prints the adjusted caps, including year of death 2026 is $88,000 and year of death 2025 is $86,000. The form also states that at least ten days have elapsed and that no application or petition for appointment of a personal representative is pending or has been granted in any jurisdiction.

Source: Colorado Department of Revenue form DR 2712, Affidavit for Collection of Personal Property Pursuant to Small Estate Proceeding

Does a living trust avoid probate in Colorado?

A revocable living trust holds the assets you title in the trustee's name. The successor trustee handles those assets. Property still titled in your own name can be a probate matter.
