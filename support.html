Support by email.

Write to plan@taxfreetrusts.com. There is no phone line.

Write to the desk

plan@taxfreetrusts.com

Include the packet you bought, the email you used at checkout and your question. Never send a Social Security number, a bank account number or a card number by email.

Printing the packet

Finish the planner and use the print button on step 5. Choose letter size paper. To keep a digital copy, choose Save as PDF in the print window.

Lost answers

Your answers live only in the browser tab where you entered them. If you closed the tab, start the planner again.

Signing

Each state sets its own signing formalities, such as the number of witnesses and whether a notary is required. Confirm your state's rules before you sign.

Funding

The funding list in the living trust packet walks the deed and the accounts. Read how funding works.

Counsel desk

The counsel desk is the attorney introduction. We do not appear in court. We do not give legal advice. Representation begins only if a lawyer accepts the matter. Paying $790 does not, by itself, hire a lawyer.
