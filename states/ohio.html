Living trust or probate

A revocable living trust holds assets you title in the trustee's name. You can serve as trustee while you are able. At death, the successor trustee handles those assets. Property you leave in your own name can still go through the probate court. A pour-over will sends leftover assets into the trust, and those leftover assets may pass through probate first.

The probate path

Ohio Revised Code section 2113.35 gives the probate court power to deny or reduce an executor's or administrator's compensation if the fiduciary has not faithfully discharged the duties. Section 2113.03 lets the probate court, on application of any interested party and after the notice the court directs, enter an order relieving the estate from administration and directing delivery of personal property and transfer of real property. Section 2113.031 is a separate summary release from administration, also ordered by the probate court. They do not state an affidavit collection limit.

Source: Ohio Revised Code 2113.03, Release from administration

Fees

Ohio Revised Code section 2113.35 allows executors and administrators fees upon the amount of all the personal property, including the income from the personal property, that is received and accounted for by them and upon the proceeds of real property that is sold: for the first one hundred thousand dollars, four per cent; all above one hundred thousand dollars and not exceeding four hundred thousand dollars, three per cent; all above four hundred thousand dollars, two per cent. They are also allowed one per cent on the value of real property that is not sold, and one per cent on stated property not subject to administration. These fees are full compensation for all ordinary services. Section 2113.36 allows reasonable attorney fees as expenses of administration.

Source: Ohio Revised Code 2113.35, Commissions

Small estate

Section 2113.03 is a probate court release from administration if the value of the assets of the estate is thirty-five thousand dollars or less, or one hundred thousand dollars or less when a surviving spouse takes the entire estate under the will or under the intestacy and allowance rules stated in the section. Section 2113.031 is a summary release from administration. For an applicant who is not the surviving spouse, the assets may not exceed the lesser of five thousand dollars or the decedent's funeral and burial expenses.

Source: Ohio Revised Code 2113.03, Release from administration

Estate or inheritance tax

The Ohio Department of Taxation states that the Ohio estate tax was repealed effective January 1, 2013 for estates of individuals with a date of death on or after that date, and that a later sunset means no Ohio estate tax is due for property first discovered after December 31, 2021. The same pages state that the department no longer requires a tax release or inheritance tax waiver form before certain assets may be transferred.

Source: Ohio Department of Taxation, Estate Tax
