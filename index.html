06

Questions worth answering before checkout.

Is a TaxFreeTrusts draft legal advice?

A draft is a document you print and finish. Reading the site, using the planner, or paying does not create an attorney-client relationship with Hanok Ventures or TaxFreeTrusts. Drafts are not a substitute for a licensed attorney in your state.

What do the three prices buy?

$79 is a will draft. $349 is a living trust draft: revocable trust, pour-over will, and a schedule of what you intend to move. $790 is the counsel desk, an attorney introduction. Paying $790 does not, by itself, hire a lawyer.

Does the living trust cut my income tax?

A revocable living trust does not reduce federal income tax. Do not retitle an IRA into the trust. Federal estate tax in 2026 is widely reported at $15 million per person. Most households under that exemption should not buy an irrevocable tax package.

What does funding mean?

After you sign, deed the home to yourself as trustee and update bank payable-on-death instructions. Leave retirement accounts in your name. Stop before moving a large life policy. The site lists the transfers. You complete them.

How do I sign, and can I cancel?

Print the packet and sign with the witnesses or notary your state requires. Email plan@taxfreetrusts.com before the draft is delivered if you need to cancel. Once a draft has been delivered, the purchase is generally not refundable, except where the law requires a refund.
