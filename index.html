Print, sign, fund

Three steps at your kitchen table. The planner does the drafting.

  1. 1

    Print

    Answer the planner and print the draft packet with your names filled in.

  2. Signature
    2

    Sign

    Sign using your state's formalities, such as witnesses or a notary.

  3. 3

    Fund

    Move the house and the accounts into the trust, line by line, from the funding list.

A trust only controls what you move into it.

A signed trust with nothing in it keeps nothing out of probate. The living trust packet comes with a funding list, so the deed and the accounts actually move.

How funding works
